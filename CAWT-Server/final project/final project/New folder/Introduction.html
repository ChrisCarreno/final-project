Introduction

Time management is the productive process of planning and organizing the time you spend on specific tasks of your life. It can be making a schedule for the day, setting the time to work on something, or taking a step by step process with a lot of work. Whether it is related to work or personal activities, time management plays an integral part of our daily lives.


It is one of the most valuable abilities for people to learn from because by practice, it can greatly strength their productivity and efficiency when it comes to handling their daily tasks especially for those who struggle to manage their responsibilities such as people with disabilities or those stressed with tight schedules.
