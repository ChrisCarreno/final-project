The Worst Examples of Time Management

Much like there are good ways of managing time, there are also plenty of ways that time management can be executed poorly.

1. Distractions

Getting distracted is one of the most prominent things that people face in their lives. Distractions are meant to take away your attention from the goals your strive to achive in favor of doing something that puts your goals on hold. The most well-known causes of distractions tends to come from the inability to pay attention, being disinterested, and that other things may have a much greater attraction than the thing that is focused on.

2. Poor Sleep

Poor sleep can not only affect your mood, but it can also affect your health, both mentally and physically, in the poorest state possible. The lack of sleep like having three or four hours of sleep, having issues with sleep like insomnia or narcolepsy, eating heavy meals like pasta or hamburgers at midnight which can make gain weight, or drinking caffine late at night all results in poor sleep.

3. Procrastination

Whenever some people feel stressed out about the situation their in, they tend to procrastinate to cope with said situation. The most common things that people procrastinate is that they delay their time on that specific goal multiple times, not work on that specific goal in fear of trying, and that they rushed their goals like work assignments at the last minute.

4. Struggling to Meet Deadlines

One of the common struggles that people face from time to time is the inability to meet so many deadlines on time. This struggle is due to the amount of work that people have to focus on, how tight and strict certain deadlines are, and the personal issues that people have to balance as well.
